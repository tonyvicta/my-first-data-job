# Why are Kitchen returns up?

**Tool:** Excel · **From:** Alex Morgan, my manager at Larkfield Homeware Co. (fictional)

## The request
> Thanks for the March summary. Priya loved it, and it's raised a question.
>
> Kitchen returns look higher than usual and she wants to know why before she talks to the buying team. Her hunch is the courier, but nobody has checked.
>
> I've attached two files. The orders file only has a product ID, so you'll need to look up each product's category, supplier, price and cost from the product list. Then compare the months and the suppliers.
>
> Send me the figures below, which explanation the data supports, and a short note I can pass on. Flag anything you're not sure about; that's part of the job.

## What I found
- **Total Q1 revenue from Delivered orders (all categories)** £44924.05
- **Gross margin on Delivered Kitchen orders in Q1** 54.99%
- **Kitchen return rate in March** 13.53%
- **Which month had the most Kitchen returns?** March
- **Which supplier's Kitchen products had the highest return rate in March?** Hearth & Co
- **What should Priya take to the buying team?** The rise comes from one supplier: its Kitchen products' returns jumped in March while the others stayed flat.

## My summary for the team
Kitchen returns rose to about a fifth of March orders, almost all from Hearth & Co products; other suppliers and categories stayed flat, so it is not the courier.
